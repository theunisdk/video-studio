# Getting started with Video Studio

## Video Studio

This is Video Studio. You ask Claude for a video in one sentence, and you get back a finished, narrated MP4.

## Step 1 · Ask

Open the repo in Claude Code and say what you need. A marketing video for a website. A tutorial for your app. Or an explainer from your docs.

## Step 2 · Research

Claude starts with research. It visits the website, takes screenshots and reads the pages. For an app, it clicks through in a read-only browser, so nothing gets changed.

## Step 3 · Script and voice

Next, it writes a short script, and records a natural voice-over for each scene with ElevenLabs.

## Step 4 · Scenes

The scenes are built in code, with React and Three.js. Every animation is timed to the words, so things appear right as they're said.

## Step 5 · Render

You can review a storyboard first. Then it renders the video with captions, and checks the file before it hands it over.

## Your videos

Your videos stay private. They live in folders that git ignores, so only the examples are in the public repo.

## Get started

To get started, clone the repo, install it, and add your ElevenLabs key. Then just ask. This video was made exactly that way.
