# How OpenClaw works

## How OpenClaw works

Let's look at how an OpenClaw setup works. It's four parts, working together as one assistant.

## Part 1 · The software

First, OpenClaw. This is the software that runs your assistant. It receives your messages, works out what to do, and sends the replies.

## Part 2 · The computer

OpenClaw needs a computer to run on. That's Hostinger: a computer in the cloud that stays on day and night, so your assistant keeps working even when your laptop is closed.

## Part 3 · The intelligence

Next, the AI model. This is the intelligence behind it. OpenClaw passes your request to a model like Claude, GPT or Gemini, which works out the answer.

## Part 4 · The conversation

And finally, Telegram or WhatsApp. That's how you talk to your assistant, using the chat app that's already on your phone.

## All together

Put it all together. You send a message, OpenClaw picks it up on its cloud computer, asks the AI model, and the reply comes straight back to your chat.
